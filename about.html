Our Heritage & Standards

Over a Decade of Structural Precision & Master Tradesmanship

Incorporated in March 2015 (Company Registration No. 09506499), Angel Construction Services Ltd was established to bring absolute structural integrity, transparent cost management, and master craftsmanship directly to London and South East property owners.

Unlike general middlemen or disjointed subcontracting outfits, we operate as a dedicated main building contractor. From deep reinforced foundations and complex structural steelwork to hand-laid masonry, bespoke joinery, and flawless plaster finishes, our on-site teams manage every trade consecutively under strict daily supervision.

Over the past 10+ years, we have completed more than 180 verified residential projects — spanning multi-storey extensions, bespoke loft suites, ground-up turnkey houses, and total property transformations across Bexley, Orpington, Bromley, Chislehurst, Greenwich, and West London.

Our Operating Model

Dedicated Main Contractor • Seamless Architectural Collaboration

Angel Construction Services Ltd is purely a main building contractor focused on physical construction, groundwork, structural steelwork, masonry, and interior finishing. We do not operate as an architecture studio directly — instead, we maintain seamless collaboration with leading associate architectural practices.

Option 1: Build to Your Existing Plans

Already Have An Architect?

We collaborate directly with your appointed architectural practice or structural engineering consultants. We review your approved CAD drawings, handle council building control notifications, and execute the physical build to exact millimetre tolerances.

Option 2: Associate Architectural Network

Need Architectural Drawings & Planning?

We connect you with our trusted associate architectural practices who handle feasibility sketches, 3D visualizations, council planning submissions, and structural calculations — keeping architecture and construction cleanly structured with single-point communication.
