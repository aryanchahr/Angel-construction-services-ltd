Architecture, Planning & Print FAQs

Key questions regarding planning permission, Building Regulations drawings, and our collaboration structure.

Many single-storey rear extensions (up to 3m or 6m for semi-detached houses under Prior Approval) and loft conversions (up to 40m³ or 50m³ volume allowance) fall under Permitted Development rights, provided your home is not in a conservation area or subject to an Article 4 direction. Our associate practice will perform an immediate planning feasibility check and apply for a Certificate of Lawfulness from your local council to ensure 100% legal compliance.

If you already have approved planning and Building Regulations drawings prepared by your own architect, we will review them thoroughly, carry out a site survey, and provide you with a comprehensive, fixed-price itemized contract.

Planning drawings focus on aesthetics, property boundaries, exterior dimensions, and the impact on neighboring homes. Building Regulations drawings are structural, technical specifications detailing insulation U-values, steel beam loads, fire escape routes, drainage connections, and foundation depths. Both sets are required before construction can begin.

Our associate Design & Print Studio features high-speed wide-format commercial plotters capable of printing full-color or black-and-white A0, A1, A2, and A3 CAD sets on the same day. Laminated weatherproof site plans are also available for construction sites.
